Forest City Solidarity: three themes, one site

Same pages and content, three stylesheets. Pick a page and a screen size to compare A, B and C side by side.

To browse the real pages in a theme, open any page with ?theme=a, ?theme=b or ?theme=c on the end. Screenshots were taken in a sandbox without Google Fonts, so headings show fallback fonts; Oswald, Source Serif and Space Grotesk load on the live site.
